## Assignment requirements

The supplied **ITU Project assignment and instructions 2026**, under its AI rules,
allows consultation, explanation and small, specific generated fragments. It
prohibits generation of larger units, complex project sections and the overall
solution. It also requires detailed understanding of all code and report text,
which is checked during the oral defence.

The supplied document gives no 10% allowance and prescribes no special AI marker
syntax. Its separate attribution rules require author names in source headers
and report headings. Disclosure and understanding do not themselves override
the restriction on larger generated sections. This audit records contribution;
it does not establish compliance or an accepted exception.
